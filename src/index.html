    LET’S MAKE IT SIMPLE.

    A few good questions.

    Where can I see the laptops in Agra?

    AMC Infotech Solution is based in Sanjay Place, Agra. Use the area map below and confirm the exact shop pin with the store before travelling.

    Do you buy laptops from other brands too?

    Yes. Start the sell-laptop checklist for Apple, Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS, Acer, Samsung or another brand. A final offer depends on inspection and store acceptance.

    What does refurbished mean?

    A previously owned laptop prepared for another owner. Appearance, repairs, battery condition and included accessories vary. Check the exact unit’s details before purchasing.

    Can I order from anywhere in Uttar Pradesh?

    Agra is our primary focus. You can prepare a purchase enquiry for other UP cities; shipping, charges and courier availability must be confirmed by the store. The website does not currently take online payments.

    Are the prices and photos for actual stock?

    This preview uses sample prices, configurations and availability with reference model photos. Actual unit photographs, battery health, condition and final prices must be confirmed before purchase.
